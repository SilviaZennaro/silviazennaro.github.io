About me

Every edit has its own language.

I'm an Italian video editor and filmmaker with a background in directing and post-production. I work across fiction, documentary and commercial content, bringing a cinematic eye, a strong sense of rhythm and close attention to detail.

I love shaping raw footage into compelling stories, finding the balance between emotion, atmosphere and narrative.
