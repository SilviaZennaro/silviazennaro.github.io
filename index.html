When It Rains Inside

Original title: Quando piove dentro

Psychological Drama · Italian · 14' · 2025 · Color · Stereo · 2.35:1
Written and directed by Silvia Zennaro

Video coming soon.

Synopsis

When It Rains Inside tells the story of Edoardo, a young man who lives a life ruled by strict rules and obsessive routines that isolate him from the world. During a rainy day, an encounter with Linda, a young newspaper seller, pushes him out of his comfort zone.

As Edo tries to connect with others, his mind blurs the line between reality and imagination, leading him to question the possibility of change. In the end, however, he finds himself alone once again, trapped in his need for control and unable to break free from the fragile balance that governs his existence.

Director's Statement

The idea originates from the intimate sense of serenity that rain can evoke, capable of transporting us into a world of imagination and creativity, where breaking rules becomes the most natural path to follow. The story aims to explore the mental barriers we impose on ourselves without any real necessity, barriers that ultimately end up immobilizing us.
