Al Biciclàr

Short film · Italy · 2026 · 12'30"
Directed by Federico Lanzani · Edited by Silvia Zennaro

Produced by Luce Cinecittà, in collaboration with Rai Cinema · Executive production: Creativite Idea · Short Attacks!

Excerpt from the film.

Synopsis

In Ferrara, four people — a foreign rider, an old man, a thief and a student — find themselves in front of the same bicycle workshop without knowing they have something in common: each of them has already been touched, in their own way, by an encounter with Al Biciclàr, a bizarre, forgetful repairman with a disarming humanity. The workshop is a place where much more than a bicycle gets fixed.
